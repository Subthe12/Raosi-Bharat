The Perfect Tadka Sequence (Ghee Tempering Order)

1

Hard Whole Spices

Add to medium-hot ghee first (Cinnamon, Cardamom, Cloves, Bay Leaf, Star Anise). Cook 20-30 secs to infuse oil.

2

Seeds & Resins

Add Cumin, Mustard seeds, Kalonji, or Hing. Wait until seeds crackle and pop (10-15 secs) to avoid bitterness.

3

Aromatics

Add Fresh Curry Leaves, Slit Green Chillies, Ginger-Garlic paste. Sauté until raw aroma vanishes.

4

Powdered Spices

Add Turmeric, Kashmiri Chilli, Coriander powder LAST on low heat with a splash of water so powders don't burn.
